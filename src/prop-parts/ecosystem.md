## Ecosystem

The regional ecosystem covering environmental management, technology, education, social services, and regional development is diverse in scope, focus, and approach; a sample of that ecosystem follows.

| Entity | Potential Relationship |
|:---|:---|
| Tairāwhiti Environment Centre (TEC) | <ul><li>Saved can be a local partner demonstrating reuse, repurpose, and revival at scale</li><li>Saved can help TEC manage the insurmountable amount of devices coming their way</li></ul> |
| TechCollect NZ (with Noel Leeming) | <ul><li>Saved can be a local redirection for the TechCollect NZ scheme, keeping useful laptops in-region</li></ul> |
| Recycle A Device | <ul><li>Saved can provide surplus devices to volunteer refurbishment schemes such as Recycle A Device</li></ul> |
