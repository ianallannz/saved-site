The situation

The end of Windows 10 support is an e-waste disaster in the making

In an example of egregious planned obsolescence, as many as 400 million computers are on their way to the waste stream.

If we don't believe in the alternative, we will miss the opportunity to save millions of good computers for communities and businesses.

These computers can be saved.
